# Pokémon Sapphire NDS: screenshot captions

Taken in DeSmuME on v16.5 (muted), at the DS's native size: 256x192 (top screen) or 256x384 (both screens). NEW = the Gen 5-style sprite set, CLASSIC = the Gen 3 set (Birch's fourth question at the start of the game).

1. `01_title_sapphire.png` and `01_title_sapphire.gif`: The title screen. The touch screen shows Sapphire's own title scene: Kyogre behind the rippling sea, with rising bubbles and its markings glowing red (the GIF is one full loop).
2. `02_slateport_city.png`: Slateport City: the Pokémon Center beside the canal, with Team Aqua grunts queuing for the Oceanic Museum. NEW sprites.
3. `03_fortree_city.png`: Fortree City: the Gym among Black 2's own round trees. CLASSIC sprites.
4. `04_mossdeep_city.png`: Mossdeep City: Black 2's Humilau palms around the Pokémon Center. NEW sprites.
5. `05_lilycove_city.png`: Lilycove City: the Department Store, with its doors set into the wall, above the Pokémon Center. CLASSIC sprites.
6. `06_petalburg_woods.png`: Petalburg Woods: tall grass among Black 2's trees. NEW sprites.
7. `07_route113_ash.png`: Route 113: volcanic ash falls on the ash-covered grass by the Glass Workshop. CLASSIC sprites.
8. `08_meteor_falls.png`: Meteor Falls: surfing at the foot of the waterfall, with Team Aqua waiting below. NEW sprites.
9. `09_underwater_dive.png`: Dive: a ray of light over a seaweed-lined trench on the seabed. NEW sprites.
10. `10_sootopolis_kyogre_storm.png`: Kyogre's storm: heavy rain under a dark sky over the Sootopolis Gym. NEW sprites.
11. `11_battle_tower_pwt.png`: The Battle Tower island: the Hoenn Battle Tower, with the Pokémon World Tournament beside it. CLASSIC sprites.
12. `12_archie_battle_throw.png`: Team Aqua Leader Archie's battle on Mt. Chimney: Brendan throws his Poké Ball. NEW sprites.
13. `13_kyogre_battle_throw.png`: The battle with Kyogre in the Cave of Origin: Brendan throws his Poké Ball. CLASSIC sprites (Sapphire's own back sprite).
14. `14_pokedex_area.png`: The Pokédex AREA page on the Hoenn map: where Wingull lives (both screens).
15. `15_trainer_card.png`: The trainer card, with Brendan's NEW picture (both screens).
