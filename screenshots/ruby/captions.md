# Pokémon Ruby NDS: screenshot captions

Taken in DeSmuME on v16.5 (muted), at the DS's native size: 256x192 (top screen) or 256x384 (both screens). NEW = the Gen 5-style sprite set, CLASSIC = the Gen 3 set (Birch's fourth question at the start of the game).

1. `01_title_ruby.png` and `01_title_ruby.gif`: The title screen. The touch screen shows Ruby's own title scene: Groudon behind the rippling sea, with rising bubbles and its markings glowing (the GIF is one full loop).
2. `02_slateport_city.png`: Slateport City: Team Magma grunts queue for the Oceanic Museum, past the market and the Poké Mart. CLASSIC sprites.
3. `03_fortree_city.png`: Fortree City: tree houses on stilts and the Pokémon Center among Black 2's trees. NEW sprites.
4. `04_mossdeep_space_center.png`: Mossdeep City: the Space Center, palm trees and the island's cliffs. CLASSIC sprites.
5. `05_route119_bridge.png`: Route 119: a bridge over the river below a waterfall, deep in the forest. NEW sprites.
6. `06_route113_ash.png`: Route 113: ash covers the trees and the grass. NEW sprites.
7. `07_victory_road.png`: Victory Road: bridges over the cave floor. CLASSIC sprites.
8. `08_underwater_dive.png`: Dive: a path of seaweed along a trench on the seabed. CLASSIC sprites.
9. `09_sootopolis_groudon_drought.png`: Groudon's drought: harsh sunlight and drifting dust over the Sootopolis Gym. NEW sprites.
10. `10_pwt_battle_tower.png`: The Pokémon World Tournament next to the Hoenn Battle Tower. NEW sprites.
11. `11_magma_hideout.png`: Magma Hideout: a Team Magma grunt spots Brendan. NEW sprites.
12. `12_maxie_battle.png`: Team Magma Leader Maxie challenges Brendan on Mt. Chimney. NEW sprites (Maxie's battle sprite by joojishibuki).
13. `13_groudon_battle_drought.png`: The battle with Groudon in the Cave of Origin: its Drought turns the sunlight harsh.
14. `14_pokedex_area.png`: The Pokédex AREA page on the Hoenn map: where Zigzagoon lives (both screens).
15. `15_trainer_card.png`: The trainer card, with Brendan's CLASSIC picture (Sapphire's art) (both screens).
